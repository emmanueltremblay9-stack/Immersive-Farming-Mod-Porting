# Immersive Farming Mod Porting

Unofficial NeoForge 1.21.1 modernization of Etyl's Immersive Farming, with Immersive Cooking & Farming integrated into the same jar for local testing.

## Included Systems

- Sprinklers, high-pressure sprinklers, irrigation, treated water, crop disease, compost fertility, and industrial composter work from the Immersive Farming port.
- Immersive Cooking multiblocks: grill oven, cookpot, food fermenter, and food processor.
- Juice fluids and buckets under the preserved `immersivecooking` namespace.
- Optional recipe compatibility for Farmer's Delight, Farm & Charm, and Vinery.
- JEI recipe categories when JEI is installed.

## Requirements

- Minecraft 1.21.1
- NeoForge 21.1.x
- Immersive Engineering is recommended/expected for the multiblock gameplay surface.

## Important Notes

Do not install the original Immersive Cooking jar together with this merged jar. The Immersive Cooking code and resources are already packaged inside this mod and preserve the `immersivecooking` IDs.
