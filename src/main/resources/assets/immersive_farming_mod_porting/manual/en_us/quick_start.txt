Quick Start
From vanilla field to engineered field

1. Craft Compost from bone meal and a compostable item, or build the Industrial Composter for bulk production.

<&compost>

2. Till dirt with a hoe to create Soil. Better hoes apply more till in one use. Crops on Soil need maximum till before they can grow.

3. Add water. Vanilla water hydrates Soil, but Treated Water also clears disease.

<&sprinkler>

4. Place a Sprinkler as a two-block-tall device and fill it with water or Treated Water through its fluid handler. A normal sprinkler covers radius 6. The high pressure sprinkler covers radius 12 and consumes more fluid.<np>

5. Plant crops, watch for disease, and use Compost on Soil when you want the next growth cycle to accelerate.
