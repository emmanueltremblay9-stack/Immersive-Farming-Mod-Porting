Grill Oven
Three-slot smoking
<&grill_oven0>

The Grill Oven is the simple cooking multiblock. It exposes item handling everywhere, has three cooking slots, and uses a fourth slot for smoking fuel.

Each cooking slot accepts items with a vanilla smoking recipe. Fuel is consumed when work is available. The output replaces the input stack in the same slot when cooking completes.

Because the Grill Oven delegates to vanilla smoking recipes, data packs and mods that add smoking recipes automatically expand its usable inputs. It does not use FE.
